Nuvio TV Launcher

A Home screen for Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV: your apps, Live TV guide, sports, calendar, recordings, YouTube (OneTube) and Music in one launcher. It is a fork of Nuvio One.

The app does not host, store or distribute media, and ships with no channels, playlists or content sources.
