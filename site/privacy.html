Privacy Policy

Nuvio TV Launcher, last updated November 19, 2026. Contact: open an issue. Home

Nuvio TV Launcher is a client application for playing media from sources you provide. It does not host, store or distribute media.

Account

The app signs you in with a Nuvio account. Authentication is handled by Nuvio's service. The app receives the account identifier and email address needed to identify your account and keep your settings in sync. Your Nuvio password is never handled by this app.

Live TV setup sync

If you turn on Setup Sync, your Live TV setup (playlist and guide definitions, favorites, hidden groups, sports and calendar choices, and layout settings) is stored in a sync service keyed to your Nuvio account, encrypted at rest, and returned only to devices signed in to the same account. You can stop syncing or delete the saved copy in Settings, Live TV, Setup Sync.

Google Drive (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for permission to see your email address (to show which account is connected) and to create and manage only the files it creates itself in your Drive (the drive.file scope). It uses this to save and restore a backup of your Live TV setup, and to store Music uploads in a folder the app creates.

  • The app can access only files and folders it created. It cannot read your other Drive files.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored on your device only. Backup files go to your own Google Drive and nowhere else. We do not receive, store or see your Google data.
  • Google user data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive in the app at any time, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Nuvio TV Launcher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Diagnostics

The app may offer optional debug or playback reports. They are sent only when you choose to send them. Personal data is never sold.
